2026 · Currently Building

ACESAutonomous Crop Evaluation Scout

ACES is a ground rover built to take crop inspection off the farmer's hands. It roams a field autonomously, capturing images of plants as it goes. When it spots a plant that looks unhealthy, it runs the image through a trained machine-learning model to identify the probable disease. The rover then packages the disease name together with the precise location of the affected plant and sends it straight to the receiver's phone — so a farmer knows what is wrong and exactly where, without walking the entire field. This one is still very much in progress.

How It Works
  1. 01Roam.The rover navigates the crop field autonomously, covering rows without manual driving.
  2. 02Capture.An onboard camera photographs plants along the route.
  3. 03Detect.Unhealthy-looking plants are matched against a trained ML model to determine the probable disease.
  4. 04Locate.The precise location of the affected plant is recorded alongside the diagnosis.
  5. 05Report.Disease name and location are sent directly to the receiver's phone.
